What happens next

Three steps, none of them a sales call.

  • Simone reads what you sent and, if you included photos, looks at those first.
  • She comes back with a straight answer: what she would specify, roughly what it costs and whether a visit is worth your time.
  • If it is, you agree a time for the home visit or office survey. There is no obligation at any point.
