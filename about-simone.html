About Simone

Very Well Hung is Simone. She visits your home, helps you choose the fabrics, headings and styles that suit the room, measures every window herself, and fits the finished result with care. Handmade made-to-measure curtains, blinds, shutters and soft furnishings, for homes and workplaces across London.

Whether it’s a bay window at home or a glare problem across an office floor, the service is the same: Simone looks at the windows, listens to what you need, and recommends the right solution, then measures and fits it properly.
